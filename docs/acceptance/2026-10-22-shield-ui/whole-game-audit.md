# Shield presentation audit

Existing shields absorb settled damage and owner economy reports the remaining amount. Their internal buff was not in the generated public buff catalog, so world nameplates could not show the shield. This batch makes the bounded shield an explicit public native-only catalog entry and carries its existing payload through visible entity snapshots and ABI33. Generic metadata drives a separate shield bar and amount/deadline text, with normal visibility budgets and retirement. No combat balance changes or renderer damage simulation.

Remaining systems: team economy compensation, more announcements, channels/special cast geometry, summon replica lifetime, dedicated artwork. Original MVP33/44 and 11 external evidence gaps remain.
