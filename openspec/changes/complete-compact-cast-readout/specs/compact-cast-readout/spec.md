# Compact cast readout requirements

## Single-line readable status

Every ability slot SHALL show active cast seconds or distinct cancelled/completed labels without character wrapping or font shrinking. Positive remaining times below one tenth SHALL display <0.1s. Full descriptive status SHALL remain in the ability tooltip.

## Retained layout and authority

Card width SHALL account for actual text at the selected scale and existing responsive composition SHALL reflow cards. Status SHALL use the current validated authoritative cast instance and SHALL clear upon retirement. Native real-viewport evidence SHALL cover narrow and wide windows, enlarged text and all status variants.
