# World channel visual

## Authoritative state

The world channel visual SHALL use only disclosed canonical channel total/remaining and a nonzero opaque 64-bit key for the matching living visible hero. Invalid, missing or ambiguous rows SHALL fail closed. Native frame epoch/tick/sequence admission SHALL reject stale and foreign changes. Legacy total-unknown buffs SHALL not infer a duration.

## Bounded lifecycle

Each actor SHALL have at most one native channel effect in the existing bounded shared pool. Current updates SHALL reuse the effect and not restart pulse age. Pool pressure SHALL drop boundedly and fresh state SHALL recover when capacity returns. Clear, hide, death, generation/epoch replacement, opt-out, reset and higher-priority recall SHALL retire the effect. Reveal alone SHALL not replay old state.

## Presentation

Elapsed fraction SHALL control ring geometry from authoritative snapshots only. Local time MAY pulse opacity but SHALL not advance progress or imply gameplay completion. Pause SHALL freeze the pulse. Valid typed channel callbacks SHALL not add a duplicate generic ring. Actual shader captures and production ABI parsing SHALL be verified on one computer.
