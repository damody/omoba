# Requirements

- Received healing modifiers apply once to direct, script and regen healing.
- Invalid factors, overflow, negative heals and dead recipients cannot manufacture HP.
- Visible enemy unit-target items can author an optional bounded reduction and publish it as a normal buff.
- Rejections do not spend cooldown or alter buffs; expiry/death and strongest refresh are deterministic.
- Fountain and hard-reset restoration retain their documented restoration semantics.
