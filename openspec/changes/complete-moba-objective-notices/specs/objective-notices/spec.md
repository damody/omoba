## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: public objective milestone state
The authority SHALL publish bounded objective notices from credited committed damage, independent of target visibility.

#### Scenario: simultaneous objectives
- WHEN multiple objective lives end in one tick
- THEN notice order is canonical, a visible notice remains uninterrupted, and at most seven recent notices wait.

#### Scenario: invalid lifecycle
- WHEN an action is anonymous, allied, repeated, forced retirement, paused or outside active play
- THEN it does not manufacture another objective success.

#### Scenario: presentation lifetime
- WHEN a public tuple is incomplete, duplicated, invalid, expired or terminal
- THEN client and native HUD display no objective notice.
