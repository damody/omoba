## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Self sprint transaction
Ready equipped sprint SHALL grant bounded timed movement only to its living authenticated owner, then spend slot cooldown. It SHALL preserve base speed, commands and controls. Pause SHALL freeze lifetime/cooldown. Other sprint sources SHALL share strongest-family aggregation and independent expiry. Invalid context, shape, definition, resource or cooldown SHALL publish neither effect nor cooldown mutation.

### Requirement: Authored native contract
Balance and localized identity SHALL come from shared authoring. Native self-input/tooltip SHALL describe bonus, duration, aggregation, no cleanse and no collision bypass. Tests SHALL use actual formal60Hz catalog input/generated native metadata without a new network match.
