## Requirements

- Critical feedback SHALL derive from actual admitted physical HP loss, not animation intent or damage magnitude.
- Native damage and primary native/typed ordinary projectiles SHALL retain the existing critical roll and clear inherited callback/visual/child entitlement.
- Missed, fully shielded, zero-physical, stale/dead or unsupported layer targets SHALL emit no critical indicator.
- Public feedback SHALL identify only the currently disclosed target/epoch and deduplicate by one-shot identity.
- Unreal SHALL use the existing bounded floating-text pool and retire on normal expiry, fog and scope/life reset.
- Actual native CRIT pixels SHALL be captured and inspected; prior reviews retain original provenance.
