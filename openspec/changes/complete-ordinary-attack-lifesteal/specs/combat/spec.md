## Requirements

- Shared Lifesteal SHALL affect explicitly classified ordinary attacks and SHALL NOT infer eligibility from damage amount/profile.
- Projectile shared leech SHALL be immutable at launch and SHALL use exact live source generation at settlement.
- Shields, overkill, packet immunity, misses/evasion and received-healing reduction SHALL determine actual healing through serial HP loss.
- Inherited children, visual shots, ordinary unclassified script skills and stale/mismatched origins SHALL NOT gain shared leech.
- Existing explicit native leech flags SHALL preserve their semantics; shared and explicit rates SHALL not double count.
- Fresh single-machine acceptance SHALL bind full source, native captures, failures and original Windows process retirement.
