# Requirements

- The impact point is the sector apex; attack direction defines orientation only.
- Geometry is bounded and deterministic with explicit quantized angular semantics and inclusive boundary/apex rules.
- Invalid rank/scalars/source geometry emits no damage or passive state; active execution of this passive is rejected.
- Candidate ordering and generations are canonical. Only living positive-HP enemies are damaged once per hook.
- Existing committed projectile origin and reward attribution remain operational after source retirement.
- Authored damage percentages and radius remain unchanged; native metadata/tooltip disclose the actual rule.
