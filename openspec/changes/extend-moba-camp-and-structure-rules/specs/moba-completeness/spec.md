## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Bounded camp groups
Camp members MUST share aggro/reset state, maintain independent combat/vitals and kill credit, and respawn only after the entire camp is cleared. Public presentation MUST resolve every member identity.

### Requirement: Recoverable leash
The authority MUST use deterministic patience and soft/hard reset phases. In-range attackers MAY reengage a soft reset; hard resets MUST reject damage. Pause and terminal phases MUST freeze private state.

### Requirement: Authored base unlock
The compiled map MUST retain its declared unlock policy. Damage admission, acquisition and public structure status MUST use that same policy.

### Requirement: Readable cast preview
An active preview MUST show locally known range/target suitability, distance and confirmation/cancellation instructions without claiming backend acceptance. It MUST retire on all existing preview cancellation and owner retirement paths.
