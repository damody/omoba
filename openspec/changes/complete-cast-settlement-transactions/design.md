# Design

Wrap successful managed casts and summoning legacy casts in serialized native Outcome envelopes with captured caster identity, rank, faction, optional owner and expected mana. Delay cast cues and aggression until admission. Expand ordinary effects in original order; preflight the complete bounded formation with friendly occupancy and immutable stats snapshots before inserting any body. Formation effects are restricted to caster resources/facing and summons so no intervening terrain/life mutation can invalidate the plan. Reject unsupported mixed formation effects, nested envelopes and stale identities. Rejecting retires only uncommitted reservations. Later same-batch casts with a rejected mana dependency fail conservatively; new inputs may retry next tick.

All script mana writes, including non-cast callbacks and on_tick, use a guarded ScriptManaCommit row containing every changed entity's original/final pool and the original ordered mana notifications. Validate all original pools and life identities before changing any pool or publishing any notification. This closes stale non-cast writes after a rejected cast, without a refund correction or hero-name exception. Membership checks use a set, not a notification-by-owner quadratic scan. Existing explicit raw ScriptSetMana remains a compatibility/native primitive; production script resource transactions emit guarded rows.
