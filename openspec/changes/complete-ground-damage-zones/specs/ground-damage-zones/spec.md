# Requirements

- First pulse after interval, exact final pulse before expiry; pause freezes, refresh restarts, remove/death stops.
- Fixed center; current exact-circle living enemy membership on every pulse; no allied/self/building damage.
- Stable source/key/pulse/target order, no lost catch-up pulses; bounded checked payloads.
- Existing raw defense/shield/profile/credit settlement applies once.
- Existing flame assault initial blast and stun remain; authored rank metadata describes additional zone damage.
- Failed admission emits no zone, damage or success cue and does not consume resources.
