# Requirements
- Missing policy SHALL retain nearest targeting; unknown or malformed policy SHALL reject authoring.
- OwnerFocus SHALL require exact owner generation, live hero, matching positive player ownership and faction, and active AttackTarget.
- The preferred target SHALL pass the same disclosed live hostile in-range attackability checks as fallback targets.
- Actual generated summon projectiles SHALL follow formal owner attack commands and return to nearest selection when that command is cleared.
- Compiled/runtime content and generated native catalog hashes SHALL remain coherent.
