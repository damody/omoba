- A caster-local restoration side effect may accompany a valid selected-target consumer; alone it still requires no target.
- Wrong shape, conflicting allegiance, missing/nonfinite/out-of-bound extras and existing exclusive effect compositions remain rejected.
- Admit all effects before resource publication. A bad ally/life/range or managed cast failure cannot leave mana, HP, cost or cooldown changes.
- Metadata cost is paid once; caster receives capacity-limited restoration and the selected ally receives healing. No target mana restoration is implied.
- Preserve stable identities and existing healing balance, use authored per-rank mana/description, and verify generated/runtime coherence plus formal60Hz resource/cooldown/replay behavior.
