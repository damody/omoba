# Requirements

- Client payload SHALL contain only two current opaque replica references, bounded point shape and closed operation.
- Only authenticated current selective protocol sessions SHALL seal references; no canonical client alias SHALL be admitted.
- Shared authority SHALL retain exact owner, life and summoned relationship checks.
- Projected acknowledgement SHALL contain only projected actor/target and redacted command shape; aliases, canonical leaks and missing identities SHALL fail closed.
- A post-step summon ACK SHALL NOT move or cancel a hero, nor invent private summon behavior in a filtered replica.
- UE command exposure SHALL remain pending until its typed issuing UI is implemented.
