## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Bounded configurable outer tower plates
Formal production and role plans SHALL enable configurable timed outer tower plating. Legacy omission SHALL disable it. Invalid rules SHALL fail setup atomically.

### Requirement: Once per life threshold rewards
Positive committed damage SHALL consume every newly crossed fixed-point HP threshold once per entity generation, including lethal multi-plate hits. Healing, duplicate or uncredited damage SHALL NOT permit later reclamation. Each plate SHALL split equally among nearby living attacking heroes with independent floor rounding.

### Requirement: Temporary authority defenses
Broken plates SHALL add normal BuffStore armor before subsequent attacks. Expiration, retirement and match end SHALL remove it. Ledger and rules SHALL participate in deterministic digest, and earned gold SHALL flow through normal owner economy projection.
