## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Bounded deterministic tower focus
Lane towers and nexus towers SHALL increase committed attack damage against the same hero using validated configurable percentage rules. First hits SHALL use base damage. Other targets, loss of legal target, timeout or entity retirement SHALL reset focus. Pausing SHALL freeze simulation deadlines. Policy and focus state SHALL participate in replay hashing without becoming enemy disclosure data.

### Requirement: Current disclosed cooldown details
Learned ability tooltips SHALL distinguish valid current remaining cooldown from total cooldown. Becoming ready SHALL retain the current total; invalid or unlearned snapshots SHALL NOT retain old details. No inactive preparation metadata, channel duration or authority ACK SHALL be inferred.

### Requirement: Whole formal game inventory
The project SHALL record implemented evidence and remaining gaps across controls, skills, hero progression, economy, map/vision, lanes/buildings, jungle/objectives, lifecycle and presentation, distinguishing local-verifiable work from external acceptance.
