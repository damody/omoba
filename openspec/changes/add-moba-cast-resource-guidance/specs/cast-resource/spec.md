## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Disclosed rank resource guidance
Learned ability tooltips SHALL show a known finite nonnegative mana cost. Unknown or invalid metadata SHALL clear prior values. Pending ability previews SHALL report insufficient mana only from a current owned alive hero with valid supported mana and known cost. Guidance SHALL NOT gate command submission or represent authority acceptance.

### Requirement: Camp attacks respect wall LOS
Camp leaders and members SHALL NOT commit attacks through opaque geometry. Blocked attacks SHALL preserve their attack budget and follow normal chase navigation, allowing later attacks when LOS clears.
