# Requirements

- Channel recovery SHALL be explicitly authored and restricted to supported typed host execution, never inferred from skill names or tooltips.
- Begin SHALL require low living-owner health, affordable exact host cost and no disclosed hostile combatant within a bounded radius.
- Missing resources/metadata SHALL reject this intention; ordinary unknown channel classes SHALL remain unsupported.
- Hidden positions/health SHALL not affect planning. Root SHALL permit the stationary cast, while silence/pause and prior channel waiting SHALL retain their contracts.
- An optional existing-hero recipe SHALL exercise the real formal player input and cost/pulse/cooldown lifecycle with deterministic replay.
