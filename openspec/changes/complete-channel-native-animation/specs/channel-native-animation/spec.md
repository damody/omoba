# Common skeletal channel presentation

The native actor SHALL select channel animations through shared semantic bindings and the configured generic fallback policy. Valid public state SHALL sample exact normalized progress in an existing clip and SHALL hold between authoritative updates. Neither local mesh time nor delayed snapshots SHALL imply completion.

Current life/identity/world-scope/frame ordering SHALL precede side effects. Death/respawn, recall and cast preparation SHALL have priority over channel; channel SHALL have priority over ordinary locomotion and hit/cast transient cues. A current clear SHALL restore the latest ordinary state. Hide/rebind/reset SHALL retire cached state; reveal alone SHALL not replay it.

Validation SHALL include two actual skeletons and real pose images, while preserving public ABI/VFX regression. No hero/camp/item-name branches or new game rules are permitted.
