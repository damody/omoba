# Requirements

- Native attacks SHALL read current shared evasion and source miss modifiers for property-only bodies, Units and Heroes.
- Existing Unit/Hero base-defense precedence and legacy critical behavior SHALL remain.
- Avoidable hits SHALL use deterministic independent dodge/miss streams; zero and one endpoints SHALL be exact.
- Avoided hits SHALL emit no damage, healing or successful direct-combat fact. Ordinary ability and true-damage flags SHALL retain bypass behavior.
- Actual lane AI at production60Hz SHALL demonstrate full evasion, full miss and restored attacks after removal; no true network match SHALL be created.
