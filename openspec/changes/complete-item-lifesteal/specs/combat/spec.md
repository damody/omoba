## Requirements

- Equipment SHALL author a finite normalized lifesteal rate in0..1, with zero backward-compatible omission.
- Real generated shop purchases/recipe consumption, sale and undo SHALL rebuild the aggregate shared passive without stacking stale leases or restoring HP.
- New-life finalization SHALL restore retained equipment passives; zero inventory SHALL remove them.
- Ordinary damage SHALL reuse actual physical HP-loss and hostile non-building target policies, existing healing reduction and source life checks.
- Generated native metadata and the actual item tooltip SHALL expose the same rate and restrictions, with inspected screenshot evidence.
- Native custom catalog admission SHALL validate the same bounds; a positive net lifesteal benefit SHALL invalidate purchase undo after combat even when attack damage is unchanged by the recipe.
