## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Self control cleanse item
The authority SHALL allow an equipped ready self-cleanse item to remove only canonical stun/root/silence from its living owner through the common BuffStore cleanse operation, including while those controls are present. It SHALL preserve unrelated effects/foreign entities/commands, require ordinary item admission and spend cooldown even when no matching controls exist. It SHALL grant no immunity. Invalid admission SHALL mutate neither effects nor cooldown.

### Requirement: Accurate self input and disclosure
The generated native catalog SHALL identify a no-target self item with accurate localized removal/cooldown guidance. Existing public status snapshots SHALL show removal and any newly reapplied control normally. Tests SHALL use real authored catalog and formal60Hz item input without a new network match.

### Requirement: Common active item context
All item transactions SHALL reject paused worlds and, when a match is present, non-playing or mismatched roster contexts before changing effects, cooldown or commands. Existing no-match owner/life semantics and Stop admission SHALL remain unchanged.
