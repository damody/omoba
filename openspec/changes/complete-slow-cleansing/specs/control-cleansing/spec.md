# Control cleansing

Shared cleansing SHALL remove current recognized generic ability and canonical projectile slows, stun, root and silence. It SHALL preserve arbitrary voluntary stat penalties, unknown/malformed/mixed slow payloads, DoT, healing reduction, shields and positive modifiers. Normal removal SHALL maintain reverse indexes and retained integration clocks. Cleansing SHALL grant no immunity and SHALL keep existing no-target input, ownership, cooldown and pause rules. Item and summoner descriptions SHALL match these boundaries.
