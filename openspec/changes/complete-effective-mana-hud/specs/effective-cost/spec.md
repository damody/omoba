# Effective mana cost

## Requirement: current scoped authority price
Owner HUD costs SHALL derive from the same checked multiplier/product as authority admission. Metrics SHALL be team-scoped, current-tick, unique and bound to ability slot/catalog/rank. Missing, invalid and free SHALL remain distinct. No raw modifier/private buff state SHALL be disclosed.

### Scenario: discounted skill
A 45-base-cost skill discounted to22.5 SHALL show22.5 and permit an owner with30mana to submit. Marked-up or invalid costs SHALL not be admitted using the static base cost. Backend remains the final admission authority.
