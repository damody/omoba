## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Public authority segment metadata
Living enabled outer towers SHALL expose initial and remaining plates with finite expiration through public buff snapshots, without granting extra combat modifiers. Healing SHALL NOT restore lost segments. Expiry, retirement and terminal state SHALL clear the metadata.

### Requirement: Generic validated native presentation
The frontend SHALL decode bounded integer segment metadata for the exact disclosed live generation, reject ambiguous sources and malformed data, and draw native segments with numeric counts and authority remaining time. It SHALL retain ordinary HP display and existing preference, retirement and frame replacement behavior. Renderer time SHALL NOT infer missing authority state.
